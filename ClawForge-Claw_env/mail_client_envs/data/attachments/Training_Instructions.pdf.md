# Annual Compliance Training Instructions

**Department:** Human Resources
**Due Date:** June 30, 2026
**Required for:** All employees

## Overview
All employees must complete their annual compliance training by the due date. This training is mandatory and part of our regulatory requirements.

## Training Modules

### Module 1: Data Privacy (30 minutes)
- Understanding personal data
- GDPR and CCPA compliance
- Data handling best practices
- Reporting data breaches

### Module 2: Workplace Safety (25 minutes)
- Emergency procedures
- Ergonomics and workstation safety
- Harassment prevention
- Incident reporting

### Module 3: Code of Conduct (20 minutes)
- Professional behavior expectations
- Conflict of interest policies
- Use of company resources
- Social media guidelines

## Instructions
1. Log into the HR Portal at hr.company.com
2. Navigate to "Training" > "Annual Compliance 2026"
3. Complete all three modules in order
4. Pass each module quiz with 80% or higher
5. Download your completion certificate

## Important Notes
- Training takes approximately 75 minutes total
- You can pause and resume at any time
- Modules must be completed in one session
- Save your certificate after completion

## Compliance Consequences
- Employees who fail to complete by June 30 will receive a formal warning
- This may affect your annual performance review
- Managers will be notified of incomplete trainings
