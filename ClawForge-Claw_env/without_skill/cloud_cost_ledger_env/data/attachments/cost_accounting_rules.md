# Cloud Cost Accounting Rules

- Approved catalog for the current reporting cycle: `cat_apac_q2_2026_current`.
- Archived catalogs are reference-only and must not be used for the official monthly report.
- Compute metrics are billed on a 730-hour monthly baseline.
- Storage metrics are billed on provisioned monthly quantities.
- The official report scope includes clusters with `cluster_role = business`.
- Shared platform clusters such as `cluster_shared_ops` must stay out of the business cluster report.
